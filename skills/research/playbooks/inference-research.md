### Inference research

**Use when:** the task is investigating model or inference behavior — why a model does what it does, characterizing a behavior, probing a hypothesis about model internals or outputs.

> Stub — flesh out with the actual steps you follow once this has been used a few times. Placeholder shape below.

1. State the question precisely — what observation prompted this, and what would count as an answer.
2. Design the smallest experiment that could falsify the hypothesis, not just confirm it.
3. Run it, capture raw outputs alongside the interpretation, not just the conclusion.
4. Write up the finding as a cited artifact (what was run, what was observed, what it does and doesn't establish).
