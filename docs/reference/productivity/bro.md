# bro

## What it does

Restates the last message in plain language, no jargon — one human talking to another
instead of an AI reaching for its usual vocabulary.

## When to reach for it

Whenever a reply came out stiffer or more jargon-heavy than it needed to be. Type
`/bro` explicitly — it never fires on its own.

## FAQ

**Why user-invoked only, instead of firing automatically when a reply sounds
jargon-heavy?**
Jargon is often intentional and precise in a technical reply — auto-firing would
second-guess correct technical language. Left to explicit judgment instead.

## Signals

- **Working:** the restated version reads like something you'd actually say out loud
  to a person.
- **Not working:** it just swaps in a few simpler synonyms but keeps the same stiff
  sentence structure.
